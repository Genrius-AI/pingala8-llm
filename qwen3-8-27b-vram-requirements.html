Qwen3.8 27B VRAM requirements: which GPU do you need?

Genrius AI guide. October 25, 2026. About 7 minutes.

Will it fit your GPU? Normal vs Pingala-8 calculator

In short

  • Qwen3.8-27B has 27.78 billion parameters. On paper that is about 55.56 GB at FP16, 17.00 GB at Q4_K_M and 8.27 GB at IQ2_XXS.
  • Its KV cache is small for its size: only 16 of its 64 layers keep one. At 32,768 tokens it is 2.30 GB, against about 8.6 GB for a plain 64-layer model like Qwen3-32B.
  • A 24 GB card (RTX 3090, RTX 4090) holds Q4_K_M with a 32k context. A 16 GB card needs a 2-bit file. On 8 GB it does not fit on the GPU at all.
  • All numbers here are memory estimates from the public config. We have not run this model.

Qwen3.8-27B is the dense 27B model in Qwen's 2026 line. At 27 billion parameters it sits right at the edge of a single consumer GPU, so the usual question is simple: which card, at which quantization, with how much context? This page answers it with arithmetic from the model's own config file, Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B config.json @ 1d4bf0f, using the same formulas as our GPU fit calculator.

Memory per quantization

Two things take most of the memory: the weights, which depend on the quantization, and the KV cache, which grows with the context. Here are both for the four formats people ask about most.

Memory estimates from public configs, not measurements: we have not run this model. Weights = parameters x bits per weight / 8, with llama.cpp's whole-file bits per weight (FP16 16.0005, Q8_0 8.5008, Q4_K_M 4.8944, IQ2_XXS 2.3824, from the Llama-3.1-8B table in llama.cpp's tools/quantize/README.md). KV cache at 16-bit, context padded to 256 cells as llama.cpp does. GB = 109 bytes. Add about 1.5 GB for the runtime's compute buffers and your desktop. These are the calculator's formulas.

As a check on the estimate, unsloth's published files (unsloth/Qwen3.8-27B-GGUF) are 29.05 GB for Q8_0, 16.46 GB for UD-Q4_K_M and 7.27 GB for UD-IQ2_XXS. They are a little smaller than our rows because the parameter count above includes the vision encoder and the multi-token-prediction layer, which a text GGUF leaves out or ships separately (the vision projector, mmproj, is 0.93 GB at BF16), and because unsloth's "UD" files mix quant types per tensor.

The largest of the four formats in the table above whose weights plus 16-bit KV cache leave at least 1.5 GB free on the card; "tight" means it fits but with less than 1.5 GB left. Card memory from NVIDIA's spec pages, taken at face value. On paper, not tested on these cards.

The pattern is simple. On 24 GB, Q4_K_M fits at every context in the table, and at 32k tokens about 4.7 GB is still free before working buffers. Q8_0 at about 29.5 GB needs a 32 GB RTX 5090 and is tight there at 32k. On a 16 GB card, 17 GB of Q4_K_M weights alone do not fit, so you either drop to a 2-bit file or keep some layers in system RAM with llama.cpp's -ngl, which is much slower. On 12 GB only a 2-bit file fits, and it gets tight at 32k. On 8 GB even the 2-bit file is larger than the card.

Why the KV cache is so small: hybrid attention

Qwen3.8-27B is a hybrid. Its config lists 64 layers in layer_types: 48 are linear-attention layers and 16 are full-attention layers (one in every four, full_attention_interval = 4). Only the full-attention layers keep a cache that grows with every token. Each has 4 key-value heads of size 256, so the cache costs 2 x 4 x 256 x 2 bytes = 4,096 bytes per token per layer, or 65,536 bytes per token for all 16.

The linear-attention layers keep a fixed state instead, the same size at 100 tokens or 100,000. llama.cpp stores it in 32-bit floats (src/llama-hparams.cpp, n_embd_r and n_embd_s): per layer, (conv kernel 4 - 1) x (2 x 16 key heads x 128 + 48 value heads x 128) for the convolution plus 128 x 48 x 128 for the recurrent state. Over 48 layers that is 156,893,184 bytes, 0.16 GB, included in every KV number on this page.

The result: 0.69 GB at 8,192 tokens, 8.75 GB at 131,072 and 17.34 GB at the full 262,144-token window. A non-hybrid 64-layer model with 8 key-value heads of size 128, the shape of Qwen3-32B, needs 262,144 bytes per token, four times as much. For long context on one card, that difference matters more than the quantization.

Settings that help

With llama.cpp, -ngl 99 keeps every layer on the GPU and -c sets the context. -fa on -ctk q8_0 -ctv q8_0 stores the full-attention cache at 8 bits, which roughly halves that part (the fixed state stays 32-bit). Start with the context you really need: at 32k the cache is only 2.30 GB, so on a 24 GB card Q4_K_M is the safe default.

Pingala-8 and this model

Pingala-8 is a 2-bit format at IQ2_XXS size. A Pingala-8 build of Qwen3.8-27B is not released yet and its quality is not measured; the size would be about the IQ2_XXS row above, about 8.27 GB of weights. Request it here: open the GPU fit calculator and press Request, or open a GitHub issue with the model, your card and the context you need.

What we have measured is on other models. On Qwen3-4B, Pingala-8 is 1.24 GB with a WikiText-2 perplexity of 16.64 against 28.61 for IQ2_XXS at the same size; on Qwen3-14B it loses narrowly to IQ2_KT (9.57 against 9.40). The released files run only in our patched llama.cpp on NVIDIA RTX 30, 40 or 50 series cards; stock llama.cpp, Ollama and LM Studio cannot load them. Details are in the paper and the benchmark dataset.

Questions people ask

How much VRAM does Qwen3.8-27B need?

On paper about 55.6 GB of weights at FP16, 29.5 GB at Q8_0, 17.0 GB at Q4_K_M and 8.3 GB at IQ2_XXS, plus a KV cache of 0.69 GB at 8,192 tokens and about 1.5 GB of working buffers. These are estimates from the public config, not measurements.

Can Qwen3.8-27B run on a 16 GB GPU?

Not at Q4_K_M fully on the GPU: the weights alone are about 17 GB. A 2-bit file of about 8.3 GB fits with a 32k context on paper, or you can keep some layers in system RAM, which is much slower.

Can an RTX 4090 run Qwen3.8-27B?

Yes, on paper. Q4_K_M weights of about 17 GB plus a 32,768-token cache of 2.3 GB leave about 4.7 GB of the 24 GB for working buffers.

Is there a Pingala-8 version of Qwen3.8-27B?

Not yet. A Pingala-8 build would be about the IQ2_XXS size, about 8.3 GB, but it is not released and its quality is not measured. You can request it from the calculator or in our GitHub issues.
